# Cryptocurrency Investment Strategies

## When to Use
- Market is in a sideways or bear state and you want to accumulate positions
- You have profitable positions and want to take profits
- Market is moving sideways without hitting new highs for extended periods

## Prerequisites
- Chart view against stable asset (USD/USDC), NOT vs Bitcoin
- Identification of a recent high
- Existing profitable crypto positions (for profit-taking)

## Strategy 1: Value Cost Average™ (Accumulation)

### Determine Market State and Trigger
| Market State | Drop Threshold from Recent High |
|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Sideways | -20% |
| Bear (Down) | -30% |

### Execution Steps
1. **Initial Buy**: When price drops to the threshold above
2. **Subsequent Buys**: Buy again if price moves down a further -10% from the high (laddering down)
3. **Position Sizing**: Increase investment amount by not more than 25% for each subsequent buy
4. **Preparation**: Draw lines in advance or place orders in advance based on these percentages

### Speculative Exception (High Risk)
For coins outside top 10 (risk of zero): Only buy when it has dropped AND you see it going up again. Do not keep buying into a drop.

### Mindset
Embrace volatility; do not get emotional.

## Strategy 2: Profit Taking (Bull Market)

### Long-term Hold Strategy
- Do not sell initially; allow winners to run for maximum upside potential
- Avoid stop losses early on due to high volatility
- Rationale: Cryptocurrencies can be used as currency without selling to gain value

### Profit Taking Execution
1. **Trigger**: When profit exceeds 100%
2. **Action**: Set a stop loss just below the current price to sell a portion (20-30%) of holdings
3. **Goal**: Take money off the table to rotate into other cryptos at lower prices

## Strategy 3: Range Trading (Sideways Market)

### Condition
Price continuously goes up and down (sideways) without hitting new highs for months/years.

### Execution
1. Trade within the 'channel' of high and low prices
2. Buy near the bottom, sell near the top
3. Keep the main amount invested, but trade a smaller portion within the range
4. Rationale: Generates profit while waiting for the eventual upturn

## Constraints
- Do not use Value Cost Average™ for speculative coins outside top 10 unless specific conditions met
- Never invest more than willing to lose entirely
- Requires market monitoring for range trading
