# Cryptocurrency Risk Assessment

## When to Use
- User intends to invest in cryptocurrency
- Initiating a cryptocurrency transaction
- Analyzing long-term value or viability of major cryptocurrencies

## Prerequisites
- Ownership of cryptocurrency
- Intent to transact or invest
- Understanding of market saturation

## Financial Risks

### Market Environment
- The industry is totally new and unregulated
- If hacked, there is no compensation

### Volatility
- Due to uncertainty, price swings of 30%+ in a single day (up or down) are not uncommon

### Investment Rule
Only invest money you are willing to lose.

### Lack of Governance
- There is no Central Bank governing Bitcoin
- No one guarantees its minimum valuation
- If a major group of merchants discards Bitcoin, value falls tremendously, leaving investors in a major loss position

### Transaction Irreversibility
- Cryptocurrency transactions are typically not reversible
- Once money is sent to an address, you cannot get it back
- Unlike traditional currency (e.g., Visa) where you can call a bank to get money back for non-delivery of services, cryptocurrency has no bank to negotiate or help
- If a merchant doesn't send items or you are hacked, there is no authority to contact, no way to reverse the transaction, and no compensation

### Verification Step
Double check that you are sending coins to the right address and in the right currency.

## Scarcity and Viability Logic

### Fundamental Assumption
A cryptocurrency is fundamentally defined as a "scarce digital commodity".

### Value Source
The value of this commodity derives directly from its scarcity.

### Market Saturation Assessment
- Since the summer of 2017, "10,000s new sources" of this digital commodity have been launched
- The introduction of these vast new sources causes the entire concept of scarcity to erode
- As scarcity erodes, the fundamental value of the commodity begins to erode

### Viability Requirement
For the best-known currencies (specifically Bitcoin and Ether) to maintain their viability:
- The market must undergo a correction
- "Many of these new cryptocurrencies will need to fail" to restore scarcity and value to the dominant players

## Constraints
- Does not apply to traditional fiat currencies with central bank backing
- Does not apply to blockchain technology utility (which is distinct from the currency)
- Applies specifically to the valuation of the commodity, not the technology
