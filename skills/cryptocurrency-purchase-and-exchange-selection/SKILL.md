# Cryptocurrency Purchase and Exchange Selection

## When to Use
- Executing a buy order for cryptocurrency
- Selecting a trading platform for the first time
- Deciding which exchange to use for fiat-to-crypto or crypto-to-crypto transactions

## Prerequisites
- Bank account
- Identification documents (ID)

## Core Process

### 1. Apply the Reserve Currency Rule
You must buy Bitcoin or Ethereum with fiat money first. Use BTC/ETH to buy other Altcoins.

### 2. Choose Exchange Type

**Option A: Fiat Exchange** (e.g., Coinbase, Gemini)
- Function: Buy/Sell crypto for fiat (USD, Euro, GBP)
- Process: Open account, verify ID, exchange fiat for Bitcoin

**Option B: Coin Exchange** (e.g., Binance, Kraken)
- Function: Connects buyers/sellers, charges fees for transactions
- Usage: Transfer BTC from Fiat Exchange here to buy Altcoins

### 3. Evaluate Selection Criteria

| Criteria | Considerations |
|----------|---------------|
| **Reputation** | Check reviews from professional traders and industry websites |
| **Fees** | Understand deposit, transaction, and withdrawal fees (Coinbase Pro has lower fees than standard Coinbase) |
| **Payment Methods** | Credit/Debit Card: Requires ID verification, high fees. Wire Transfer: Takes longer (bank processing time) |
| **Verification** | Most US/UK platforms require ID for deposits/withdrawals (Anti-Money Laundering). May take several days |
| **Exchange Rate** | Rates vary by exchange (up to 10% or more). Always shop around |

### 4. Popular Exchange Examples
- **Kraken**: Large, safe, has banking license
- **Binance**: Largest in world, most currency pairs, lowest trading fees

## Constraints
- Account opening is simple but not easy (convoluted for beginners)
- Must buy BTC/ETH first as 'Reserve Currency' to buy other Altcoins
