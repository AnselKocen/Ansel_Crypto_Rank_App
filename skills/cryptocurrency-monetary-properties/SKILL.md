# Cryptocurrency Monetary Properties Assessment

## When to Use
Use this skill when:
- Evaluating cryptocurrency as money or a store of value
- Assessing digital assets for investment purposes
- Analyzing cross-border trade capabilities
- Comparing digital assets to traditional stores of value

## Prerequisites
- Market acceptance of the cryptocurrency
- Digital wallet infrastructure availability

## Assessment Procedure

### 1. Evaluate Scarcity

Assess the scarcity principle:

- Check if there is a maximum supply limit
- Verify the issuance schedule and how new units are created
- Identify deflationary mechanisms that reduce supply over time

**Key indicator**: A strictly limited supply (e.g., 21 million cap) indicates stronger scarcity properties.

### 2. Assess Digital Gold Properties

Evaluate three key properties:

**Storage**: Ease of secure storage with minimal maintenance requirements

**Divisibility**: Ability to subdivide into smaller units for precise transactions

**Transport**: Cost and speed of transfer across borders

| Property | Ideal Characteristics |
|----------|----------------------|
| Storage | Digital storage, minimal maintenance |
| Divisibility | High divisibility (e.g., 8 decimal places) |
| Transport | Low cost, fast global transfer |

### 3. Analyze Value Appreciation Potential

Consider long-term value factors:

- **Supply dynamics**: Controlled or decreasing supply increases appreciation potential
- **Adoption rate**: Growing user base supports value growth
- **Halving events**: Periodic supply reductions (if applicable)
- **Projected timeline**: Estimate when final supply will be reached

### 4. Evaluate Universality

Assess global acceptance and independence:

- **Global accessibility**: Can it be used anywhere, anytime?
- **Fee independence**: Is it free from exchange rates, transaction charges, and country-specific fees?
- **Regulatory independence**: Is it immune to currency issues, bank authorizations, or regional restrictions?

### 5. Verify Decentralized Control

Confirm user control and network consensus:

- **User sovereignty**: Users control their accounts and operations
- **Peer consensus**: Decentralized network maintains balance and transaction integrity
- **No central authority**: Avoids unknown rules imposed by physical banks

## Constraints

- Value fluctuates based on market demand
- Dependent on network infrastructure availability
- Market acceptance is not guaranteed
- Regulatory environments vary by jurisdiction

## Expected Output

An assessment of the cryptocurrency as:
- A global, scarce, and efficient medium of exchange
- A store of value with appreciation potential
- A universally accessible asset independent of traditional financial systems
