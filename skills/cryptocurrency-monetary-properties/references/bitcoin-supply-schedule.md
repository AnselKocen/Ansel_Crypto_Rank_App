# Bitcoin Supply Schedule

## Key Metrics

- **Maximum Supply**: 21 million BTC
- **Final Supply Year**: Approximately 2140
- **Block Reward Halving**: Every 210,000 blocks (~4 years)

## Historical Block Rewards

| Era | Block Reward | Period |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| Genesis | 50 BTC | 2009-2012 |
| First Halving | 25 BTC | 2012-2016 |
| Second Halving | 12.5 BTC | 2016-2020 |
| Third Halving | 6.25 BTC | 2020-2024 |
| Fourth Halving | 3.125 BTC | 2024-2028 |

## Supply Curve Characteristics

- Supply increases at a decreasing rate
- Approximately 94% of all Bitcoin will be mined by 2030
- Final 1% will take over 100 years to mine
- Deflationary by design due to lost coins and decreasing issuance

## Comparison with Physical Gold

| Property | Bitcoin | Physical Gold |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| Supply Cap | 21 million (fixed) | Unknown (depends on mining) |
| Divisibility | 8 decimal places | Requires melting/refining |
| Transport | Digital (minutes) | Physical (days/weeks, high cost) |
| Storage | Digital wallet | Physical vault/security |
| Verification | Blockchain ledger | Assay/testing required |

## Value Appreciation Factors

1. **Stock-to-Flow Ratio**: Measures scarcity relative to annual production
2. **Halving Cycles**: Historically correlated with price appreciation
3. **Adoption Metrics**: Active addresses, transaction volume, institutional holdings
4. **Network Effects**: Metcalfe's Law suggests value grows with user base squared
