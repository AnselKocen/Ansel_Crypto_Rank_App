# Early Money Systems Evolution

## When to Use
- Analyzing the origins and development of money
- Examining pre-metallic currency systems
- Studying historical barter and commodity exchange
- Researching anthropological or economic history of money
- Classifying historical currency types by material and era

## Core Analysis Framework

1. **Identify the exchange system type**
   - Determine if the system is barter, commodity money, or ceremonial exchange
   - Assess the social and economic context

2. **Classify by historical period**
   - Pre-history (barter origins)
   - Ancient agricultural period (livestock, grain)
   - Early civilization period (shells, standardized commodities)
   - Indigenous ceremonial systems (potlatch, wampum)

3. **Analyze key characteristics**
   - Material composition of the currency
   - Geographic origin and spread
   - Social function (exchange, status, ceremony)
   - Duration of usage

## Classification Categories

### Barter Systems
- Direct exchange of resources or services
- Requires mutual advantage between parties
- Predates formal money systems

### Commodity Money
- Livestock (cattle, sheep, camels)
- Agricultural products (grain, vegetables)
- Natural materials (shells)
- Has intrinsic value beyond exchange

### Ceremonial Exchange
- Gift-based systems with social hierarchy implications
- Establishes and reinforces social rank
- Often involves competitive displays of generosity

## Output Format
Provide a historical classification including:
- Currency type and material
- Time period of usage
- Geographic origin
- Social and economic function
- Historical significance
