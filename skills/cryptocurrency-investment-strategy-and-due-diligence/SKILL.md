# Cryptocurrency Investment Strategy and Due Diligence

## When to Use
Use this skill when:
- Developing investment strategies for cryptocurrency
- Evaluating potential cryptocurrency investments
- Performing due diligence on new projects
- Identifying red flags in potential investments
- Explaining different ways to generate income from crypto

## Income Generation Methods

### 1. Mining

#### Proof of Work (PoW)
- **Process**: Use computational power to verify blockchain transactions
- **Reward**: Receive Bitcoin or other coins
- **Requirements**: Energy intensive, solve complex puzzles
- **Profitability**: Limited for average person (high competition, costs)

#### Proof of Stake (PoS)
- **Process**: Stake coins to gain voting power
- **Reward**: Earn transaction fees and block rewards
- **Advantage**: Reduced energy consumption
- **Scaling**: Larger holdings = greater power

### 2. Lending
- **Process**: Traders borrow your coins for margin/leverage trading
- **Return**: Borrowers return coins with percentage commission
- **Risk**: Default risk, platform risk
- **Platforms**: Various crypto lending platforms available

### 3. Buying Established Cryptocurrencies
- **Process**: Purchase existing assets like Bitcoin or Ethereum
- **Strategy**: Long-term holding or active trading
- **Risk**: Market volatility, regulatory risk
- **Examples**: BTC, ETH, major altcoins

### 4. Buying New Offerings (ICO/IEO/STO)

#### Types
- **ICO** (Initial Coin Offering): Direct to investors
- **IEO** (Initial Exchange Offering): Through exchange
- **STO** (Security Token Offering): Regulated security tokens

#### Potential Returns
- High returns possible (e.g., £1,000 → £10,000-£100,000)
- Short timeframes possible
- **Risk**: Extremely high, many scams

## Due Diligence: Red Flags to Avoid

### 1. "Me-too" Coins
- **Sign**: Coins that do exactly the same as existing coins with no added benefits
- **Action**: Avoid
- **Exception**: Consider if significantly better/faster/cheaper than existing solutions

### 2. Industry-Specific Coins
- **Sign**: Coins designed for just one market (e.g., Potcoin for marijuana)
- **Reasoning**: No real NEED if general coins (Bitcoin, Monero, Ethereum) can solve the problem
- **Action**: Avoid single-use case coins without clear advantage

### 3. Team Evaluation
- **Red Flag**: Team too small or unqualified
- **Requirements**:
  - Relevant experience to execute whitepaper
  - Large enough to handle project
  - Plans to hire if needed
- **Action**: Avoid one-man bands or inexperienced teams

### 4. Trading Volume Analysis
- **Red Flag**: Low trading volume (not enough buyers/sellers)
- **Requirement**: Look for ever-increasing volume as market matures
- **Risk**: Low volume = illiquid, hard to exit positions
- **Action**: Avoid low volume coins

## Due Diligence Checklist

### Whitepaper Review
- [ ] Clear problem statement and solution
- [ ] Technical feasibility
- [ ] Realistic roadmap and milestones
- [ ] Token utility clearly defined

### Team Background
- [ ] Relevant experience in blockchain/tech
- [ ] Track record of successful projects
- [ ] Transparent team profiles
- [ ] Adequate team size for project scope

### Market Analysis
- [ ] Clear target market and use case
- [ ] Competitive advantage over existing solutions
- [ ] Growing trading volume
- [ ] Community engagement and support

### Technical Assessment
- [ ] Working product or MVP
- [ ] Code audit results
- [ ] Security measures in place
- [ ] Scalability considerations

## Risk Management
- Diversify across different strategies
- Never invest more than you can afford to lose
- Start with established cryptocurrencies before new offerings
- Perform thorough due diligence on all investments
- Be prepared for high volatility and potential total loss
