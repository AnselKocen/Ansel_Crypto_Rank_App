# Cryptocurrency Fundamentals and Blockchain Mechanics

## When to Use
Use this skill when:
- Defining what cryptocurrency is
- Explaining how blockchain technology works
- Describing the mining process
- Introducing Bitcoin and its properties
- Comparing different types of digital assets

## Core Concepts

### Cryptocurrency Definition
Cryptocurrencies are digital currencies that:
- Transfer money without intermediaries (banks, Visa)
- Use encryption technology (origin of "crypto" name)
- Operate on a peer-to-peer decentralized network
- Utilize a public ledger (blockchain) for all transactions
- Execute transfers faster and cheaper than traditional systems

### Classification Terms
- **Altcoin**: Any coin competing with Bitcoin (Dash, Litecoin, Dogecoin)
- **Digital Currency**: Virtual currency, usually unregulated, used within specific communities
- **Cryptocurrency**: Currency based on cryptography, using digital signatures, operating on decentralized networks

**Relationship**: Most cryptocurrencies are digital currencies, but most digital currencies are NOT cryptocurrencies (lack cryptographic/decentralized elements).

### Blockchain Mechanics

#### Decentralized Ledger System
- Network powered by users without central authority
- Thousands of computers worldwide display and update the ledger
- Every transaction verified repeatedly by all computers
- Updates refresh every few minutes

#### Consensus Mechanism (51% Rule)
- If disagreement occurs, majority (51%) determines valid information
- Valid information updated across all computers
- Ensures network integrity

#### Immutability
- Transactions documented like a fly trapped in amber
- Each new transaction adds a layer
- Past transactions become increasingly difficult to change
- Information becomes irrefutable evidence

### Mining Process

#### Proof of Work (PoW)
- Miners confirm, record, verify, and disperse transaction information
- Must prove work by committing computer power to solve puzzles/mathematical functions
- Proof elements: power, time, effort, heat, computation
- Reward: Fraction of cryptocurrency for each completed transaction

#### Proof of Stake (PoS)
- Created to reduce energy consumption
- Stake coins to gain voting power
- Larger holdings = greater power

### Scarcity Principle
- Value derived from scarcity (like Gold vs. pebbles)
- Bitcoin scarcity enforced by:
  - Mining difficulty (requires computational power and time)
  - Hard cap: Maximum 21 million Bitcoins

### Bitcoin Characteristics
- First ever cryptocurrency and blockchain pioneer
- Solves problem of digital money duplication and manipulation
- Decentralized peer-to-peer network without central authority
- Fixed supply: 21 million Bitcoins (all mined by ~2140)
- Impossible to counterfeit or inflate
- Known as "digital gold standard" or "Liquid Gold"
- Users have ultimate control without third parties

## Key Variables
- **Consensus Threshold**: Percentage required to validate (typically 51%)
- **Max Supply**: Hard limit of tokens (e.g., 21,000,000 for Bitcoin)
- **Mining Input**: Computational resources (power, time, effort, heat)
