# Investment Risk Disclaimer and Educational Nature Policy

## When to Use
Apply this disclaimer when:
- Providing information about cryptocurrency investments
- Discussing trading strategies or investment programs
- Referencing Investment Mastery Trading courses or YourCryptoClub membership
- Analyzing ICOs, STOs, or IEOs
- Any context where financial decisions might be made based on provided information

## Core Principles

### 1. Educational Nature
- All information is strictly for **educational purposes only**
- Examples are for **educational and illustrative purposes only**
- No specific recommendations for particular cryptocurrencies or blockchain technology are made

### 2. Professional Status
- Investment Mastery Trading is **NOT** a stockbroker, broker dealer, or investment advisor
- Third-party references (exchanges, wallets, financial institutions) are for **illustrative purposes only**
- Choice of service provider is at the **total discretion of each individual**

### 3. Risk Acknowledgment
- Cryptocurrency and ICO investments are **HIGHLY speculative**
- You can **lose all your investments**
- No guarantees of results or investment returns can be made

## Required Actions

1. **State the educational nature** of all information provided
2. **Clarify professional status** - not a financial advisor
3. **Warn about speculative risk** and potential for total loss
4. **Recommend professional consultation** with a licensed broker before implementing any investment program
5. **Emphasize individual discretion** in all investment decisions

## Risk Level
- Investment Risk Level: **HIGHLY speculative**
- Potential Loss: **100% of investment possible**
- Guarantees: **None**
