# ICO/STO/IEO Investment Execution

## When to Use This Skill
Use this skill when:
- Due diligence on a cryptocurrency project is complete
- Investment decision has been made
- You need to execute a transaction for an ICO, STO, or IEO
- You need guidance on wallet setup and fund transfer procedures

## Prerequisites
- Completed due diligence on the target project
- Fiat currency available for investment
- Selected ICO/STO/IEO project identified

## Execution Procedure

### 1. Acquire Required Cryptocurrency
- Exchange fiat money for the required cryptocurrency (typically Bitcoin or Ethereum)
- Ensure you have sufficient funds for the investment amount plus transaction fees
- Note: The startup will subsequently exchange this crypto back to fiat to pay for operational costs

### 2. Set Up Online Wallet
- Create an online wallet (e.g., MetaMask at https://metamask.io/)
- **Critical**: Do not use exchange wallets directly
- Many exchanges block transfers to ICO startups
- Ensure wallet is properly secured and backed up

### 3. Transfer Funds to Startup
- Send the specific cryptocurrency requested by the startup
- Use the exact wallet address provided by the startup
- Double-check the address before sending (crypto transactions are irreversible)
- Verify transaction confirmation on the blockchain

### 4. Post-Investment Engagement
- **Active Monitoring**: Follow development progress, get involved in the community, and promote the project
- **Networking**: Attend conferences to meet the team and gain insights not available in the Whitepaper
- **Negotiation**: Leverage relationships to negotiate pre-sale discounts (e.g., remind team of conference meetings to lower minimum ETH requirements)
- **Diversification**: Spread risk across multiple investments (recommend 30+ ICOs) due to speculative and unpredictable nature of success

## Key Constraints
- Most startups do not accept crypto directly from exchange accounts
- Investments are highly speculative and unpredictable
- Always use a personal online wallet, never transfer directly from an exchange
- Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible—verify all details before sending

## Required Variables
- `crypto_type`: The cryptocurrency required (typically Bitcoin or Ethereum)
- `wallet_source`: Your personal online wallet address (not an exchange)
- `target_address`: The specific wallet address provided by the startup
